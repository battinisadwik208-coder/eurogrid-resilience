Project concept

Problem

Critical services cross borders, but crisis information is fragmented across agencies, operators, and languages. Teams lose time deciding what matters first.

Approach

EuroGrid normalises updates, maps dependencies, identifies cascading risk, and produces a human-reviewable briefing with confidence and source provenance.

Why Europe

European infrastructure is interconnected across municipalities and countries. A shared coordination layer supports mobility, health, energy, and sustainability without replacing accountable local decisions.

Prototype boundary

This demo uses a fixed scenario to show the interaction. A production build would connect approved feeds and require role-based access, audit logs, and human approval before any operational action.
